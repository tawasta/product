====================================================
Automatically parse barcode when it is in GS1 format
====================================================

Extract information to product barcode while reading a barcode
with a barcode scanner. The barcode is expected to be in GS1 format.

Configuration
=============
None needed

Usage
=====
Select a product and scan a barcode with a barcode scanner.
Barcode is expected to be in GS1 format and proper information
is extracted from the barcode.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================
This module affects onchange udpate of barcode field on product.template
model.
