=================
Product: Diameter
=================

* Adds a new field, Diameter, to products
* The field is shown after Width in the product variant and template forms
* A diameter entered when creating a product is saved to its variant
* Depends on OCA's product_dimension for UOM management

Configuration
=============
* None needed

Usage
=====
* Go to Product Variant or Template form to see the new field
